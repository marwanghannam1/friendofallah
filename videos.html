Purification of the Body · days 1–7

Day 14 min 6 sec

The Tongue

Guard the tongue from backbiting, lying, slander and idle talk, and use it for dhikr, softness and good advice.

Day 215 sec

The Ears

Guard the ears from backbiting, music and idle talk, and use them to listen to the Qur'an and reminders of Allah.

Day 332 sec

The Eyes

Lower the gaze, guard the eyes from the unlawful, and look at the world through the eyes of remembrance and mercy.

Day 410 sec

The Hands

Keep the hands from harm and the unlawful, and use them to help others, give charity and remember Allah.

Day 524 sec

The Private Parts

Guard your chastity, learn the Sunnah of purification, and practise the du'as of the toilet and of intimacy.

Day 614 sec

The Feet

Walk away from places of sin and towards the masjid and the people of Allah, as if walking with the Prophet ﷺ.

Day 732 sec

The Stomach

Avoid the unlawful and overeating, follow the Sunnah of eating, and be thankful with every bite.

Purification and Enlightenment of the Mind · days 8–13

Day 84 min 14 sec

Negating Sinful Thoughts

When a sinful thought appears: ignore it, enter the Presence of Allah, seek refuge, and turn to something wholesome.

Day 95 min 46 sec

Communion with Allah

Every hour refresh the Presence of Allah, and speak to Him from the heart during everyday activities.

Day 101 min 51 sec

Deep Thinking

Each day, draw and write down 7 lessons from the events and things you encounter.

Day 112 min 12 sec

Act on Good Thoughts

Whenever a good thought comes to mind, act on it or make a plan to act on it.

Day 122 min 10 sec

Good Opinions

Recall 3 good qualities in every person you meet and ask how you can develop them in yourself.

Day 133 min 8 sec

Serving Without Expectation

Serve the creation without expecting anything from them, placing all expectation in Allah alone.

Enlightenment of the Heart · days 14–31

Day 143 min 4 sec

Sincerity for the Akhirah

Do every major good deed for success in the Akhirah and to avoid punishment.

Day 151 min 48 sec

Meeting & Parting for Allah

When meeting and departing from anyone, refresh the intention that you meet and part for Allah.

Day 163 min 11 sec

Worship Out of Love

Perform good deeds because of who Allah is, out of love, and to emulate His beautiful qualities.

Day 1711 min 41 sec

Allah's Majesty

After every good deed, recall that we can never fulfil the right of Allah's greatness and majesty.

Day 181 min 24 sec

Reflecting on Blessings

Reflect on your blessings and realise you can never thank Allah enough for them.

Day 194 min 15 sec

Serving Others

Serve those you sit with, and believe that other Muslims are better than you.

Day 202 min 43 sec

Attributing Goodness to Allah

Attribute every good act you perform to Allah's grace and mercy, not to yourself.

Day 2137 sec

Giving Preference to Others

Give preference to others in all matters, e.g. when queuing up or with food.

Day 229 min 21 sec

Attributing Blessings to Allah

Assign every blessing, physical and spiritual, to Allah; even your effort is from Him.

Day 233 min 16 sec

Recounting 10 Blessings

Every day recount 10 blessings and use each in a way that pleases Allah.

Day 242 min 43 sec

Thanking the Means

Every day express gratitude to 3 of the people or means through whom you receive a blessing.

Day 255 min 53 sec

Thanking Allah for Allah

Recall 7 names and attributes of Allah and thank Him for who He is.

Day 261 min 59 sec

Praying for Those You Wronged

Every day make du'a for anyone you have harmed, knowingly or unknowingly, that Allah blesses them with better.

Day 272 min 47 sec

Forgiveness for Ingratitude

Recall 7 blessings you have not been thankful for and seek forgiveness for that ingratitude.

Day 286 min 33 sec

Modesty in Presence

Refresh hourly that Allah is watching and hearing you, and do not use your body in a way that displeases Him.

Day 297 min 26 sec

Patience in Difficulty

When calamity or difficulty strikes, refuse any sinful means of fixing the problem.

Day 303 min 55 sec

Helping Others

Find someone going through the same difficulty as you and help them for Allah's sake.

Day 313 min 24 sec

Your Final Prayer

Before each of the five daily prayers, remind yourself that this could be your last Salah.

Purification of the Nafs · days 32–39

Day 327 min 9 sec

Basic Desires

When fulfilling basic needs, be present with Al-Razzaq and recognise every provision is from Him.

Day 332 min 8 sec

Knowledge & Development

When learning and growing, be present with Al-ʿAlim and Al-Hakim; all knowledge is from Allah.

Day 342 min 12 sec

Acknowledgment & Approval

When the nafs craves appreciation, be present with Al-Shakur and Al-Khabir and seek approval from Allah.

Day 353 min 44 sec

Control & Power

When leading or instructing, remember Al-Qahhar and Al-Malik, the true owner of all power.

Day 3614 min 29 sec

Envy

Connect to Al-Muqsit: compare yourself only to your previous self and express your neediness to Allah.

Day 374 min 38 sec

Arrogance

Redirect arrogance: the only one you are better than is Shaytan, as Allah made you the best of creation.

Day 383 min 35 sec

Pride

When criticised or shown your faults, channel pride into reflecting on your own sins.

Day 393 min 32 sec

Showing Off

When receiving blessings or doing good deeds, seek Allah's appreciation rather than people's.

Khalwa: Intensive Training · days 40

Day 403 min 19 sec

Organise a Khalwa

Dedicate 3 days of training in seclusion to increase your spiritual connection with Allah.
