Course 1: Purpose, identity and change · 6 modules

Completing each module is essential to build a comprehensive understanding. You will grasp key concepts and develop new skills to meaningfully improve your own life and help others.

Module 143 min

What are your values?

Understanding our values is at the core of living a life of purpose. In this module, we will explore the concept of personal values, uncovering how they shape our choices, relationships, and overall fulfilment. By identifying and clarifying your values, you'll gain insights into what genuinely motivates you and how these guiding principles influence your behaviour and decisions.

⬇ Download the workbook (PDF)
Module 252 min

What is your identity?

People wage wars over identities. Just look at how dearly people value their nationality, gender, and skin colour. But what is your true identity? Are we merely advanced animals hoarding possessions and seeking praise, pleasure, and power, or is there more to who we are? Through self-reflection exercises and guided discussions, this module provides real clarity on our true selves.

⬇ Download the workbook (PDF)
Module 342 min

Setting your goals

Now that we have identified our values and understood our true identity, the next step is to start setting our life goals. Life is short and therefore striving for unbefitting goals is a waste of our precious life. In this module we will discuss the true nature of this world and how we can use this understanding to strive for ambitious lofty goals.

Module 454 min

How to achieve change

Change is an essential part of personal growth and a cornerstone of achieving a fulfilling life. It enables us to break free from stagnation, expand our horizons, and unlock our true potential. Whether it's pursuing new goals, adapting to challenges, or embracing self-discovery, making changes allows us to grow. This module explores the practical way to make real lasting changes in our lives.

Module 550 min

How to maintain balance

As we strive for success in various aspects of life, true fulfilment lies in maintaining balance. Excelling financially while neglecting our family would not be seen as true success. In this module, we explore the Seven Trees of Balance which include the Spirit, Family, Provision, Learning, Health, Social Interaction, and Service, all essential for a well-rounded and meaningful life.

Module 639 min

Practical application

In this session, everything comes together. We consolidate all that we have learnt in the previous modules and establish a clear plan of action. This practical plan is driven by the lessons taken from earlier sessions, and you will leave with a clear sense of purpose and direction. Get ready to transform your life.

Guided Meditation

This is a guided meditation where we go through a structured process.
