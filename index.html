Why become a Friend of Allah, how the 40 days work, and when to start.

What is this about?

We were created to reflect the attributes of Allah on earth. When Allah told the angels, “I am putting a representative (Khalifah) on earth,” He was speaking about us.

A Friend of Allah (wali) is someone who lives that purpose: their tongue, eyes, hands, thoughts and heart reflect His Names. It is not reserved for a few. It is built step by step, by purifying ourselves (tazkiyah) until Allah loves us.

For 40 days you train on a different part of that purification. Every morning you get one task on WhatsApp, an optional short video explaining it, and an image for your phone background to keep it in front of you all day.

The benefits in this world of being a Friend of Allah

Protection

مَنْ عَادَى لِي وَلِيًّا فَقَدْ آذَنْتُهُ بِالْحَرْبِ

“Whoever harms My friend, I will declare war against them.” [Sahih Bukhari]

Your du'as are answered

“…and if he asks Me, I will give him, and if he asks My protection, I will protect him.” [Sahih Bukhari]

No fear and no grief

“There will certainly be no fear for the Friends of Allah, nor will they grieve.” [Surah Yunus 10:62]

Days 1–7

Purification of the Body

Seven days, seven parts of the body. Guard each one from sin and use it for what pleases Allah.

Days 8–13

Purification and Enlightenment of the Mind

Clear the mind of sinful thoughts, then fill it with the presence of Allah, deep reflection and good opinions.

Days 14–31

Enlightenment of the Heart

Build the qualities of the heart: sincerity, humbleness, thankfulness, repentance, modesty, patience and remembrance of death.

Days 32–39

Purification of the Nafs

Direct every desire of the self to Allah, lawful ones through His Names, and unlawful ones by turning them around.

Days 40

Khalwa: Intensive Training

Three days of seclusion to deepen your connection with Allah through du'a and dhikr.

How to take part

  1. Join the WhatsApp communityThe task for each day is posted there every morning.
  2. Pin the chatKeep it at the top of WhatsApp so the task is always easy to find.
  3. Set a daily alarmAn alarm at 8:50am reminds you to check the task for the day.
  4. React with an emojiReact to the day's message to confirm you have made the intention.
  5. Set the day's image as your phone backgroundSo the task is in front of you every time you pick up your phone. Get the images.
  6. Share your reflectionsPost your insights on the group to encourage everyone else.
