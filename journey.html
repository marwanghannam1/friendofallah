Immersion Overview

For 40 days the seeker cycles through practices for each stage of transformation: the body, mind, heart and nafs. You train on a different aspect every day. Open any section for the full detail of each exercise.

Purification of the Body

Seven days, seven parts of the body. Guard each one from sin and use it for what pleases Allah.

Purification of the Body

Each day focuses on one part of the body: what to avoid, and what to increase.

1
The TongueGuard the tongue from backbiting, lying, slander and idle talk, and use it for dhikr, softness and good advice.
Watch the video (4 min 6 sec)
What to avoid and what to increase

Try to avoid

  1. Backbiting.
  2. Slander.
  3. Lying.
  4. Intentionally swearing falsely using one of Allah's names or attributes.
  5. Giving false witness about a past event.
  6. Carrying tales/narrations between people in order to ruin their relationships.
  7. Ridiculing or making fun of another person.
  8. Obscene talk or talk full of curses.
  9. Ascribing to Allah, His prophets, His angels or Prophet’s ﷺ, companions what they are high above and free of.
  10. Uttering black magic words in order to bring about paranormal effects.
  11. Singing or reciting sensual/sexual lewd lyrics.
  12. Praising people in their presence who are not free of arrogance and conceit.
  13. Hypocrisy i.e. being two-faced.
  14. Revealing someone’s secret.
  15. Making a false promise to someone intentionally.
  16. Argumentation and intense debate.
  17. Finding fault with what Allah have given one and cursing it.
  18. Incessantly talking about affairs that do not concern one.
  19. Making a statement about oneself that express that one is pure and fault free.
  20. Talking about the details of our belief system without knowledge.
  21. Talking to opposite gender without a permissible need.

Try to increase

  1. Using your tongue with tenderness and softness when you speak.
  2. Using tongue to do Dhikr of Allah.
  3. Only speak when it is necessary to do so and do not waste the breath which Allah Azawajal has given you.
  4. Use your tongue to give advice (naseha) to help one another through this life.
  5. Always have a smile through hardship or ease, so that when people see you they shall see the ways of life of Holy Prophet ﷺ.

Detailed example: how the tongue reflects the Names of Allah

2
The EarsGuard the ears from backbiting, music and idle talk, and use them to listen to the Qur'an and reminders of Allah.
Watch the video (15 sec)
What to avoid and what to increase

Try to avoid

  1. Eavesdropping. Listing to someone's private talk.
  2. Listening to conversation of opposite gender without a permissible need.
  3. Listening to music and musical instruments with inappropriate lyrics.
  4. Listening to backbiting.
  5. Listening to idle talk.
  6. Listening to hateful speech.

Try to increase

  1. Use your ears to listen to Qur’an and Ahadith.
  2. Use your ears to listen to things which remind you of Allah and the hereafter.
  3. Whenever you hear a sound, divert your attention towards Allah. Use different sounds/vibration to ponder upon the blessings of Allah and the hereafter.
3
The EyesLower the gaze, guard the eyes from the unlawful, and look at the world through the eyes of remembrance and mercy.
Watch the video (32 sec)
What to avoid and what to increase

Try to avoid

  1. Safeguarding from looking at the opposite gender.
  2. Safeguarding from unlawful images in magazine/ videos/ on the internet or on TV.
  3. Safeguarding from looking at materialistic things which take you out of the remembrance of Allah.
  4. Gazing excessively at Duniya (worldly materialistic things), wishing to attain it so deeply.
  5. Looking with jealousy, hatred, or in a belittling way.

Try to increase

  1. Lower your gazes when looking at even lawful things.
  2. Train your gaze to obey you, don't let it drift in different directions.
  3. Try not to look with wide eyes, using full view when looking at lawful things.
  4. Whenever you observe the world, think about Allah and the hereafter. Be it the blessings of Allah or the reminders of the hereafter.
  5. Use your eyes to see through the eyes of remembrance.
  6. Look through the eyes of mercy. Be forgiving towards each other. Everyone has his own shortcomings but forgiveness can be a tool to encourage and help one another. (See beyond the faults and look at the light of his/her goodness e.g. husband/wife).
  7. When you open and close your eyes, observe and feel the qualities of 99 name of Allah. Live as though you live through what the Holy Prophet ﷺ saw and felt. Through blessings or hardship, you will always become clear of your objective. Every blink is a new start, every blink you see and ponder upon the blessed names of Allah. Every blink, you awaken in awaiting to see Allah. Every blink is intense want of meeting the Creator and His beloveds. A meeting which you wish every time you blink.
4
The HandsKeep the hands from harm and the unlawful, and use them to help others, give charity and remember Allah.
Watch the video (10 sec)
What to avoid and what to increase

Try to avoid

  1. Causing harm to others with hands.
  2. Stealing.
  3. Touching Non-Mehram.
  4. Writing lies.
  5. Writing hurtful notes.

Try to increase

  1. Use your hands to help others in need.
  2. Give charity.
  3. Use your hands in lawful means.
  4. When touching permissible things, be in a state of thankfulness (Shukr): as you are blessed with hands and a feeling of touch. Even if you do not have this ability, still be thankful that Allah has saved you from unlawful use. One's movement should be used to fulfil the rights of Allah and the creation.
  5. Keep your hands in the presence and remembrance of Allah.
  6. Let every touch and feeling reflect the hereafter e.g. softness of a pillow is a sign and remembrance of the ease and comfort of the hereafter. The heat and fire should remind you of the hellfire.
  7. Every time you write, remember and reflect on the book of deeds (Aamaal) which angels write your deeds on. So have mercy upon people but at the same time be firm in this world upon the commands of Allah Azzawajal.
5
The Private PartsGuard your chastity, learn the Sunnah of purification, and practise the du'as of the toilet and of intimacy.
Watch the video (24 sec)
What to avoid and what to increase

Try to avoid

  1. Fornication: committing adultery.
  2. Homosexuality.
  3. Exposing private parts to anyone except for your spouse or for a medical examination if needed.
  4. Having intimacy during menstrual cycle.
  5. Satisfying intimacy needs through forbidden methods.
  6. Not cleaning unwanted hair.
  7. Not safeguarding yourself from the urine drops. For example using urinal and dropping urine all over your cloths.
  8. Having intimacy with your spouse using unlawful methods.

Try to increase

  1. Learn the ways of Sunnah relating to cleansing of the private parts.
  2. Thank Allah for the ease of relieving. No matter how much pain a person goes through, all this shall never compare to the punishment of the hereafter. So be thankful in all circumstances.
  3. Learn and practice the supplication (Dua) of entering and leaving the toilet. This will protect you against the devil.
  4. Learn and practice the supplication (Dua) of before and after one engages in lawful intimacy with his wife/husband. It shall become a blessed relationship which shall keep you both in the presence of Allah Azawajal. One shall not let his desires overcome him like many people have unlawfully committed sins with their wives. It shall also protect you from the devil.
  5. Cleansing is half of Eemaan. Cleansing and purification will protect you from the evil.
6
The FeetWalk away from places of sin and towards the masjid and the people of Allah, as if walking with the Prophet ﷺ.
Watch the video (14 sec)
What to avoid and what to increase

Try to avoid

  1. Going toward forbidden places.
  2. Going to meet Non-Mehram.
  3. Going to see haram movies, going for concert or going to get something to watch that is not permissible.
  4. Traveling to place of sin.

Try to increase

  1. Travel closer towards the Sunnah in all walks of life.
  2. Travel towards good places, the mosque, the people of Allah and the places if remembrance.
  3. Walk as if today is your last day.
  4. With every footstep, imagine you are walking with the Holy Prophet ﷺ, so see through every step the way of His Sunnah.
  5. Learn the supplications (Dua) of travelling.
  6. Learn the supplications (Dua) of entering and leaving a Masjid.
  7. Learn the supplications (Dua) of leaving and returning to your home.
  8. Learn to walk like the Holy Prophet ﷺ.
7
The StomachAvoid the unlawful and overeating, follow the Sunnah of eating, and be thankful with every bite.
Watch the video (32 sec)
What to avoid and what to increase

Try to avoid

  1. Smoking.
  2. Drinking alcohol.
  3. Using drugs.
  4. Eating unlawful food.
  5. Drinking unlawful drinks.
  6. Eating too much or drinking too much.
  7. Eating or drinking opposing the Sunnah.

Try to increase

  1. Follow the Sunnah while eating, drinking and siting etc.
  2. Everything you consume, be thankful with every bite and remember the life of Holy Prophet ﷺ.
  3. While eating remember those who are less fortunate and those who have nothing.
  4. Eat healthy food.
  5. Give the body the right portion of food and exercise.
  6. Your body should not be ruled by your stomach, but your heart should be the ruler and people should see the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ in you.
  7. Have a balanced exercise. Do not overwhelm your body with physical exercise as the true exercise is of the heart (purification of the heart).

  3. Level 3 · Day 34Acknowledgment and approval (to be known, appreciated, forgiven and accepted)Names: Al-Khabir, Ash-Shakur, Al-Ghaffar, Al-Ghafur. When the nafs wants appreciation for a good deed, remember Al-Khabir is aware of every action, intention and thought. If the King of kings knows, what value is a servant's awareness?
  4. Level 4 · Day 35Control and power (to be in control, followed and listened to)Names: Al-Mutakabbir, Al-Malik, Al-Qahhar. When you lead or instruct, remember how many still disobey Al-Qahhar despite His power, so who are we to force others? The real owner of all authority is Al-Malik; you are only a means to serve His command.
  5. Level 5Self-actualisation and purpose (to have purpose, plans, goals and self-awareness)Names: Al-Badiʿ, Al-Awwal, Al-Akhir. When planning, be present with Al-Awwal and Al-Akhir: you plan, but Allah is beyond time and sees what is best. Your unique gifts come from Al-Badiʿ, who originated you unlike anyone else.
  6. Level 6Love and connection (to be loved, connected and have companionship)Names: Al-Wadud, Al-Mujib, Al-Qarib, Al-Raqib, As-Samiʿ, Al-Basir. You are never alone: every word to Allah is heard and answered. Any love you receive is an extension of the love of Al-Wadud.

Tick across every day you meet the condition. If you break it, stop and start again from the beginning. The levels practised in the 40 days are highlighted.

  1. Level 1GreedE.g. greed for wealth, for knowledge, or for blessings.
  2. Level 2 · Day 36EnvyE.g. being envious of the knowledge or the blessings of others.
  3. Level 3AngerE.g. anger at criticism, attachment to name and fame, anger at ill treatment.
  4. Level 4 · Day 38PrideE.g. self-reliance in your own knowledge, or admiring your own abilities.
  5. Level 5 · Day 37ArroganceE.g. arrogance from knowledge or from authority.
