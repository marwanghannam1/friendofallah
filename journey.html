Purification of the Body

Seven days, seven parts of the body. Guard each one from sin and use it for what pleases Allah.

Purification of the Body

Each day focuses on one part of the body: what to avoid, and what to increase.

1
The TongueGuard the tongue from backbiting, lying, slander and idle talk, and use it for dhikr, softness and good advice.
Watch the video (4 min 6 sec)
What to avoid and what to increase

Try to avoid

  1. Backbiting.
  2. Slander.
  3. Lying.
  4. Intentionally swearing falsely using one of Allah's names or attributes.
  5. Giving false witness about a past event.
  6. Carrying tales/narrations between people in order to ruin their relationships.
  7. Ridiculing or making fun of another person.
  8. Obscene talk or talk full of curses.
  9. Ascribing to Allah, His prophets, His angels or Prophet’s ﷺ, companions what they are high above and free of.
  10. Uttering black magic words in order to bring about paranormal effects.
  11. Singing or reciting sensual/sexual lewd lyrics.
  12. Praising people in their presence who are not free of arrogance and conceit.
  13. Hypocrisy i.e. being two-faced.
  14. Revealing someone’s secret.
  15. Making a false promise to someone intentionally.
  16. Argumentation and intense debate.
  17. Finding fault with what Allah have given one and cursing it.
  18. Incessantly talking about affairs that do not concern one.
  19. Making a statement about oneself that express that one is pure and fault free.
  20. Talking about the details of our belief system without knowledge.
  21. Talking to opposite gender without a permissible need.

Try to increase

  1. Using your tongue with tenderness and softness when you speak.
  2. Using tongue to do Dhikr of Allah.
  3. Only speak when it is necessary to do so and do not waste the breath which Allah Azawajal has given you.
  4. Use your tongue to give advice (naseha) to help one another through this life.
  5. Always have a smile through hardship or ease, so that when people see you they shall see the ways of life of Holy Prophet ﷺ.

Purification of the Mind: all 11 levels

Tick across every day you meet the condition. If you break it, stop and start again from the beginning. The levels practised in the 40 days are highlighted.

  1. Level 1 · Day 8Purification of evil and sinful thoughts

    Aim: Protect and purify the mind from illicit thoughts that oppose the Sharīʿah.

    Method: Apply immediately when a thought appears:

    1. Ignore the thought; do not entertain it or argue with it.
    2. Enter the Presence of Allah ﷻ: bring to heart that He is watching, hearing, and with you.
    3. Seek refuge while in that Presence: Aʿūdhu billāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm.
    4. Divert your attention to a lawful, wholesome matter (work, a family duty, study, a neutral object, etc.).

    Signs of progress: Intrusive thoughts lose force; recovery becomes faster; baseline serenity increases.

    This method shows how even the thoughts of Shayṭān can be used to redirect you into the Presence of Allah ﷻ: using his own tactics against him.

  2. Level 2 · Day 9Hourly refresh of the Presence of Allah ﷻ, seeking goodness and protection

    Aim: Make living under the watchfulness of Allah ﷻ normal.

    Method:

    1. Every hour, briefly refresh the Presence: “Allah is watching me, hearing me, and with me.”
    2. During habitual actions (e.g. eating, drinking, commuting), speak to Allah from the heart: “O Allah, grant goodness in this action and protect me from harm.”
    3. If you forget to practise this every hour, simply refresh the Presence of Allah ﷻ as soon as you find yourself out of His Presence.

    Target: Perform and practise for 30 days.

    Signs of progress: The heart turns to Allah spontaneously; less heedlessness in routines.

  3. Level 3Seek Allah’s permission before needs and tasks

    Aim: Move from self-permission to Divine permission.

    Method:

    1. Before fulfilling needs (e.g. eating, drinking, resting, travelling, messaging, purchasing), seek permission from the heart: “Permission with eating… permission with drinking… permission with travelling… O Allah, allow and bless this for me.” Then proceed with Presence.

    Target: Each day, note 3 or more moments when you consciously sought permission and how it affected the action. Practise this for 30 days.

    Signs of progress: A measured pace, fewer impulsive choices, a higher degree of Presence of Allah ﷻ.

  4. Level 4Sunnah duʿās for daily actions (with Presence)

    Aim: Recite the daily Prophetic supplications with meaning and adab.

    Method:

    1. Over 1 month, learn and implement the best-known Sunnah duʿās tied to daily actions (waking, dressing, entering and leaving home, eating, travel, etc.).
    2. Continue for 2 to 3 more months, learning at least one new duʿā each week.
    3. When reciting any of the Sunnah duʿās, be in Presence, understand the basic meaning, speak to Allah from the heart, and seek permission, blessing and protection.

    Target: Recite at least 5 of the Sunnah duʿās with Presence every day.

    Signs of progress: Duʿās arise naturally; the sense of companionship with Allah increases.

  5. Level 5Tafakkur (reflection): seeing Allah’s signs in creation

    Aim: Train the heart to see creation as pointing to Allah: His Names, Attributes, Beauty and Mercy.

    Method:

    1. Each day, write 7 reflections that connect things you observe to Allah’s Names and Attributes, e.g. “The tree’s rootedness reflects al-Qayyūm (the Upholder).” “The sweetness of fruit reminds me of al-Raḥmān’s Mercy.”

    Target: 7 short entries every day for 30 days.

    Signs of progress: A habit of Allah-centred perception; gratitude and awe in ordinary moments.

  6. Level 6 · Day 10Tadabbur (deep thinking): seeking lessons and positive meanings

    Aim: Draw practical lessons from events, strengthen positive thinking, and guide your conduct.

    Method:

    1. Each day, write 7 lessons you learn from things you encounter (nature, people, work, news, successes and failures), e.g. “The tree teaches humility and shade-giving service.” “The vast sky teaches me to raise my aspiration.”

    Target: 7 lessons recorded every day for 30 days.

    Signs of progress: Quicker recovery from setbacks; proactive, more positive thinking, even in circumstances that seem negative.

  7. Level 7Overcoming suspicious thoughts (suʾ al-ẓann)

    Aim: Replace suspicion with ḥusn al-ẓann (good opinion), adab and benefit, even when receiving poor conduct.

    Method: Whenever suspicion arises about a person or situation:

    1. Pause in the Presence of Allah ﷻ.
    2. Make excuses for the other person: list at least three plausible, charitable explanations.
    3. Apply tadabbur: ask, “What lesson can I take from this for my own improvement?” Write it down.

    Target: Log each incident, your lesson, and the three excuses you made.

    Signs of progress: A cleaner inner state; less resentment; more just and merciful dealings.

  8. Level 8One hour of Presence after each ṣalāh

    Aim: Carry the fragrance of prayer into the rest of your time.

    Method:

    1. After each of the five daily prayers, remain in Presence for at least one hour.
    2. Within that hour, refresh the Presence every 15 minutes (a brief turning of the heart and remembrance).

    Target: Note the five hours after the prayers and your four refresh points in each hour, for 30 days.

    Signs of progress: The tranquillity of prayer lasts; less slipping into heedlessness after prayer.

  9. Level 9Presence before ṣalāh (the lead-in hour)

    Aim: Enter the prayer already present with Allah ﷻ.

    Method:

    1. In the hour before each prayer (or the time available, if shorter), refresh the Presence every 15 minutes leading into the iqāmah.

    Target: Record your refreshes before each prayer, and a note on how it changed your opening takbīr.

    Signs of progress: Settling quickly in prayer; deeper khushūʿ from the first moment.

  10. Level 10Presence in sleep (completing the 24 hours)

    Aim: Turn rest into worship by entering sleep in the Presence of Allah ﷻ.

    Method:

    1. Prepare with wuḍūʾ: if possible, make or refresh wuḍūʾ before sleeping, with the Presence of Allah ﷻ.
    2. Sunnah duʿās: recite the Prophetic supplications before sleep with Presence and understanding, for example: اللَّهُمَّ بِاسْمِكَ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا “In Your Name, O Allah, I die and I live.”
    3. Heart-to-heart: briefly speak to Allah from the heart before closing your eyes.
    4. Visualisation: close your eyes while imagining the Name of Allah ﷻ, settling into His Presence.
    5. During the night: if you wake or turn, recite the Sunnah duʿā with Presence: الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ (Al-ḥamdu lillāhil-ladhī aḥyānā baʿda mā amātanā wa ilayhin-nushūr): “All praise is for Allah who gave us life after causing us to die, and to Him is the resurrection.”

    Target: Practise Presence in sleep every night for 30 days.

    Signs of progress: More serenity on waking, remembrance of Allah present in dreams, and the full 24 hours turned into worship.

  11. Level 11The Prophetic level of Presence (every half hour)

    Aim: Reach the Prophetic rhythm of Presence with Allah ﷻ, connecting every half an hour as a reflection of the original 50 prayers.

    Method:

    1. Every 30 minutes, refresh and enter the Presence of Allah ﷻ.
    2. Keep this discipline continuously, adding it after completing all the previous levels of training.
    3. Practise it consistently for 30 days as an advanced stage.

    Signs of progress: Presence continues seamlessly through the waking day; the heart is naturally anchored in Allah at Prophetic intervals; life is seen as revolving around Allah’s nearness.

    The Prophet ﷺ was first commanded with 50 prayers, later reduced to 5 with the reward of 50. Twenty-five waking hours divided by 50 prayers equals a connection every half an hour. This is the original Prophetic rhythm of Presence with Allah ﷻ.

Enlightenment of the Mind: all 9 levels

Tick across every day you meet the condition. If you break it, stop and start again from the beginning. The levels practised in the 40 days are highlighted.

  1. Level 1 · Day 11Acting on good thoughts

    Encourage and act on any good thought that comes into the mind. These divine inspirations are gifts from Allah ﷻ, meant to be entertained and acted upon so that the mind fills with beneficial thoughts.

    A good thought is like a guest: the more you entertain it, the more it will visit. If a person does not entertain good thoughts, they can be deprived of being inspired by Allah ﷻ.

  2. Level 2Deepening the Presence of Allah ﷻ through His Names and Attributes

    Here the seeker moves beyond general awareness of Allah ﷻ into conscious application. Presence (ḥuḍūr) is deepened through a daily practice of reflecting on and applying the Names and Attributes of Allah ﷻ.

    Just as the believer recalls their phone countless times a day, so the seeker trains themselves to recall Allah ﷻ by His Attributes. The more one remembers His Names, the greater their value becomes in the heart, and the more the Presence of Allah ﷻ shapes their thoughts and actions.

    Each day, select seven Names of Allah ﷻ and actively apply them in your state of Presence: whenever you refresh the remembrance of Allah ﷻ during the day, refresh it through seven of His Divine Attributes, consciously placing yourself under their shade. For example:

    • When seeking guidance: Allah is watching me (Ar-Raqīb), Allah is hearing me (As-Samīʿ), and Allah is guiding me with wisdom (Al-Ḥakīm).

    • When seeking peace: Allah is near to me (Al-Qarīb), Allah is seeing me (Al-Baṣīr), and Allah is calming me as the Source of Peace (As-Salām).

    The Qur’an commands believers to “remember Allah much” (Qur’an 33:41): remembrance is not repetition alone, but awareness of how His Names are showing upon us in each moment. In time the Names leave athar, spiritual imprints, that transform the inner state, and the believer checks their heart the way people check their pocket for their phone: confirming that Allah ﷻ is with them.

  3. Level 3 · Day 12Benefiting from developing a good opinion of others

    Focus on the good qualities of others and strive to see and absorb them. This creates a positive environment and stops you dwelling on people’s negative sides.

    When meeting a person you will see both good and bad traits, but incline your focus towards the good. This is the Sunnah of Allah ﷻ: if a person has 49% bad deeds and 51% good deeds, Allah ﷻ does not look at the bad but focuses on the good they have done.

    With every person you meet, reflect on at least three good qualities, let these be your focus when dealing with them, and think: “How can I develop and increase this quality in myself?” With this mindset you are always benefiting, looking inwards and seeing the other person as a mirror for learning and self-development.

  4. Level 4Benefiting from seeing the defects of others

    Use the defects you see in others as a means of self-improvement. Reflect on how to avoid these traits yourself, and direct any negative feeling towards the sin, not the sinner.

    When a bad quality is observed, think: “How do I make sure I do not have this bad quality and fall into the same mistake?” This is a form of self-accounting (muḥāsabah) that quickly accelerates purification. It is the essence of wisdom: Sayyidunā Luqmān عَلَيْهِ ٱلسَّلَامُ said he learned wisdom by observing the actions of the foolish, and then doing the opposite.

  5. Level 5Seeing the deeper wisdom behind bad traits

    Every evil quality is really a good quality that Shayṭān steers towards the wrong ends. Sayyidunā Khālid ibn al-Walīd رضي الله عنه was a fierce warrior against the Muslims; when that same courage was directed towards Allah ﷻ, it became a rewardable quality. Many of the Awliyāʾ likewise repurposed qualities once used for the dunyā towards the path of Allah ﷻ.

    Reflect on the good quality being misused and ask: “Do I possess this same quality, but am I misusing it towards evil?”

    This mindset also lets you deal with people who have defects with Prophetic wisdom: instead of condemning them, encourage them to redirect the very same qualities towards the way of Allah ﷻ.

  6. Level 6Mindsets to benefit in the day

    Start the day with the intention to please Allah ﷻ and actively seek opportunities to do so. Refresh your intentions at set points to create ease for others and fulfil their needs.

    After Fajr: Pleasing Allah ﷻ

    Intention: That you want to please Allah ﷻ today in a way you have never pleased Him before.

    Explanation: The mind is fresh at the beginning of the day, so actively search for opportunities to please Allah ﷻ.

    Effect: When the intention is refreshed, Allah ﷻ pleases the person in ways they have never experienced before.

    After Ẓuhr: Creating ease

    Intention: Whoever you meet, intend to create ease for them for the sake of Allah ﷻ.

    Explanation: In the middle of the day, when you meet the most people, create ease for others, both proactively and in response.

    Effect: Allah ﷻ in turn creates ease for you in your own affairs.

    After ʿAṣr: Fulfilling the needs of creation

    Intention: If anyone is in need, ask Allah ﷻ to send them to you so that you can fulfil their need for His sake.

    Explanation: Sometimes chances to create ease are not obvious. By ʿAṣr, ask Allah ﷻ to arrange the opportunity to fulfil someone’s need.

    Effect: As was explained to Sayyidunā ʿAlī رضي الله عنه, this is a sign of Allah’s love: that He sends people to you to have their needs fulfilled. Few deeds bring one closer to Allah ﷻ than fulfilling the needs of His creation.

    Sayyidunā Anas ibn Mālik reported: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said, “Whoever is concerned about the Hereafter, Allah will place richness in his heart, bring his affairs together, and the world will inevitably come to him. Whoever is concerned about the world, Allah will place poverty between his eyes, disorder his affairs, and he will get nothing of the world but what is decreed for him.” (al-Tirmidhī 2465)

  7. Level 7Mindsets to benefit through the 24 hours

    In addition to the three mindsets of Level 6, implement the following three:

    After Maghrib: Gratitude for goodness received

    Intention: If anyone has done any goodness for you, acknowledge it and give thanks, however small.

    Explanation: Towards the end of the day, reflect on all the blessings received and thank those who have shown you kindness.

    Effect: Allah ﷻ increases the blessings and places more barakah in them.

    After ʿIshāʾ: Forgiveness

    Intention: If anyone has wronged you, overlook and forgive, however large the mistake. If you have wronged others, seek forgiveness.

    Explanation: Before ending the day, think of any wrongs you have experienced and consciously let them go, ending with a clean heart.

    Effect: This removes the burden of ill feelings, lightens the heart, and raises a person among the people of Paradise through forgiveness.

    Tahajjud: Donating good deeds

    Intention: Donate all the good deeds you performed during the day to the Ummah of the Prophet ﷺ and the rest of humanity.

    Explanation: This is the most intimate time with Allah ﷻ, a time of deep reflection and connection, when the spirit receives what it cannot receive at any other time of day.

    Effect: A person is saved from relying on their deeds, and instead receives a special presence of Allah ﷻ and the Prophet ﷺ. Their deeds are multiplied by the number of people they are donated to.

  8. Level 8Mindset to beautify all interactions

    The believer is like a fragrance: wherever they go, they beautify their surroundings. Whenever you interact with anyone or anything, leave it more beautiful than before.

    The Prophet ﷺ said: “The best of you are those who bring benefit to others.” This is achieved both by bringing benefit and by reducing or giving up your own rights. Just as profit in trade comes from increasing revenue or reducing costs, spiritual profit comes from doing good deeds and from giving up your rights.

    For example, a person may tidy their bed and leave a pleasant fragrance, beautifying it for when they return.

  9. Level 9 · Day 13Serving the creation without expectation

    Remove all expectations of goodness from the creation and place them solely in Allah ﷻ. This prevents disappointment and nurtures gratitude and reliance on Allah ﷻ. Two effects arise:

    1. If you receive goodness from the creation, even if small, it is beyond your expectation and so feels like a great blessing.

    2. If you receive harm from the creation, you are protected from heartbreak and oppression, because your expectations were never on people. Instead you turn to Allah ﷻ, the only true Benefactor.

  4. Level 4 · Day 35Control and power (to be in control, followed and listened to)Names: Al-Mutakabbir, Al-Malik, Al-Qahhar. When you lead or instruct, remember how many still disobey Al-Qahhar despite His power, so who are we to force others?   6. Level 6Love and connection (to be loved, connected and have companionship)Names: Al-Wadud, Al-Mujib, Al-Qarib, Al-Raqib, As-Samiʿ, Al-Basir. You are never alone: every word to Allah is heard and answered. Any love you receive is an extension of the love of Al-Wadud.
